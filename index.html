The Analyst Files · Case #10
THE FEATURE VERDICT
Social Media

Setup

The design team is excited: “We want to let people add colored gradient backgrounds to their Facebook status updates — we think it’ll boost engagement. Can we just ship it to everyone?” Your answer is not yes or no — it’s “let’s find out, properly.”

Evidence

  • “We think it’ll help” is a hypothesis, not a fact. Shipping to 100% on a hunch means if it backfires, it backfires on everyone.
  • An A/B test splits users into treatment (colored backgrounds) and control (no change) — the difference is the evidence.
  • Sample size, duration, and statistical significance are non-negotiable — not optional math.

The Mystery

The team could peek at results every day and stop the test the moment treatment looks ahead. Why is that dangerous?

The full A/B testing flow: Clarify the Goal → Pick North Star + Guardrail Metrics → Hypotheses (H₀ / H₁) → Sample Size & Duration → Run the Test → Watch for Pitfalls (Primacy, Novelty, Network Effect) → Launch Recommendation.
